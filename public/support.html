Quick answers.

How do I order a hosting plan?

Open the plans page, choose a tier, then use the Discord link to contact your team. Automated checkout is not connected in this template.

Can I upgrade my plan?

Upgrade availability depends on your real hosting capacity and billing setup. Confirm this with your hosting team before promising an upgrade.

Where do I see server status?

Visit the Status page. Its current entries are illustrative placeholders and must be connected to real monitoring before being treated as live.

How do I change prices and plans?

Edit the plans array in public/config.js. The plans page renders cards from that configuration automatically.
